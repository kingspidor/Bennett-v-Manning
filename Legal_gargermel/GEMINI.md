# GEMINI.md - Legal Analysis Context

This directory contains a comprehensive set of legal documents, research, and evidence related to the multi-case litigation of **Zackariah Bennett v. Melvina Manning**. The litigation spans over nearly two decades and involves complex issues of custodial interference, alleged perjury, fraud on the court, and domestic violence protection orders (DVPOs).

## Directory Overview

The project is organized into several key categories to facilitate legal analysis and case management:

- **`Court_Cases/`**: Subdivided by case number (e.g., Appellate Case `87007-6`, Superior Court Case `22-2-09380-3`) and general filings. It contains the core of the litigation history, including motions, orders, and briefs.
- **`Legal_Resources/`**: Contains foundational legal materials, including `Court_Rules` (RAP, CRLJ, GR), `Case_Law_and_Research` (notably on parental alienation and custodial interference), and `Forms_and_Templates`.
- **`Discovery_and_Evidence/`**: Holds evidentiary materials such as `Declarations`, `Exhibits`, `Discovery_Requests`, and `Evidence_and_Notes`. This section is critical for identifying factual disputes and alleged misrepresentations.
- **`Administrative_and_Personal/`**: Contains personal records (DCS, Financial, Indigency) that provide context for the appellant's status and ongoing administrative interactions.
- **`Archives_and_Media/`**: Stores multimedia evidence (Images, Audio) and older, unorganized folders.

## Core Legal Themes

- **Res Judicata / Collateral Estoppel**: The central procedural hurdle. The respondent argues that the 2022 DVPO hearing adjudicated the merits of the appellant's tort claims, while the appellant argues the DVPO was limited in scope to "unlawful harassment."
- **RAP 9.11 & 10.8**: Frequent use of these rules indicates an ongoing attempt to introduce "extra-record" evidence to prove "fraud on the court" and "manifest injustice."
- **Independent Torts vs. Family Law**: The appellant seeks to establish that his civil tort claims (for actions dating back to 2008) are independent of the family law custody determinations and should not be barred by them.
