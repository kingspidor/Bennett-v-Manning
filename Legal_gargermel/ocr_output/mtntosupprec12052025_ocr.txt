e Exhibit A: Facebook communications between Appellant and Respondent (2010-2019), introduced

e Exhibit B: Relevant excerpts from the June 28, 2024 summary judgment hearing transcript.

IV. GROUNDS FOR RELIEF
A. The Motion Should Be Granted as Unopposed

Respondent's failure to file a timely response constitutes a waiver of any objection to the requested
supplementation. The Court's November 24, 2025 order contemplated a response by December 1, 2025, and the

absence of such response permits the Court to grant the motion without further briefing.
B. The Requested Materials Are Essential to Appellate Review

The exhibits sought to be added were part of the trial court proceedings but were omitted from the appellate

record by error or accident. RAP 9.11(a) authorizes supplementation under these circumstances.

Exhibit A (Facebook communications) was introduced into evidence on November 16, 2023, and demonstrates
genuine issues of material fact regarding Respondent's fraudulent inducement and ongoing interference with
Appellant's parental rights. These communications are central to Appellant's arguments on appeal regarding

tolling of the statute of limitations and the existence of disputed material facts precluding summary judgment.

Exhibit B (transcript excerpts) documents the trial court's reliance on inadmissible evidence and extra-record

materials at the June 28, 2024 summary judgment hearing, demonstrating violations of CR 56(c) and (h).

Without these materials, the appellate record is incomplete and does not fairly present the issues raised on

appeal.

V. CONCLUSION

For the foregoing reasons, and in light of Respondent's non-compliance with this Court's order, Appellant
respectfully requests that the Court grant his Motion to Supplement the Record and order the inclusion of
Exhibits A and B in the appellate record.
