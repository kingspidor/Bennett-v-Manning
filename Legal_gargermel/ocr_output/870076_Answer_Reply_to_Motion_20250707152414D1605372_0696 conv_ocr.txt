STATEMENT OF THE CASE
Mr. Bennett filed this civil action in in King County Superior Court in 2023. Ms.

Manning filed a Motion for Summary Judgment in this matter. On July 3, 2024 the trial
court entered an order granting Ms. Manning’s Motion for Summary Judgement.
Bennett y. Manning, app. at Pp. 3 (2024).
In this case, the trial court found that it is undisputed that Mr. Bennett’s parental rights
had been terminated by the Snohomish County Superior Court in 2013. Bennett, app. at
p.8 and p. 9 (2013). Thus Mr. Bennett had no basis for civil action some 10 years after
his parental rights had been terminated. /d. at p. 9.

ARGUMENT
The trial court did not err when it indicated that Mr. Bennett’s complaint had been
adjudicated previously in court. /d at p. 3. Mr. Bennett made his allegations against Ms.
Manning to the court that heard her request for a domestic violence protection order.
Ms. Manning submitted to the Superior Court a copy of the declaration presented in the
DVPO case refuting Mr. Bennet’s various civil claims against Ms. Manning. Manning v.
Bennett app. at p. 10. (2022).
Mr. Bennett’s parental rights were voluntarily terminated. Bennett at p. 14. He has no

claim against Ms. Manning with respect to his biological son.

CONCLUSION
Ms. Manning respectfully requests the Superior Court’s Order granting Summary Judgment
be upheld because Mr. Bennett voluntarily relinquished his parental rights and thus does not have

a cause of action against Ms. Manning.

THIS MATTER came before the Court on Defendant’s Motion for Summary Judgment. The
Court has considered the record in this case, the materials submitted by the parties in regard to
this motion, as well as oral arguments from the parties, and the Court finds itself fully informed.
Now therefore,

ITIS HEREBY ORDERED, ADJUDGED, and DECREED as follows:

The Court finds that there are no genuine issues of material fact for trial in regard to the
issues presented and the Defendant is entitled to Summary Judgment as a matter of law.

Plaintiff’s complaint alleges that the Defendant committed tortious interference with
parental rights, both criminally and civilly. As the Plaintiff is not an elected or appointed
prosecuting attomey, Plaintiff has no lawful basis or authority as an individual to bring a
criminal prosecutorial action on behalf of the State of Washington. Further, the facts are
undisputed that the Plaintiff does not have any legal parental rights to be interfered with, as these
rights were previously terminated by the Snohomish County Superior Court in 201 3, thus

providing no basis for further civil action on these grounds. Any other issues presented in Mr,
