### THE DEMARAY FRAUD — FOR CR 60(b) AND § 1983

**Attorney:** Mark M. Demaray, Inc. P.S. — 145 Third Ave South, Suite 201, Edmonds, WA 98020

**What Demaray Did:**
1. Drafted Communication Agreement stating contact between Bennett and T.B. was in T.B.'s "best interests"
2. Agreement required judge's signature to be enforceable (§1.1) — judge never signed
3. Agreement required Bennett to notify both Court and Manning within 48 hours to withdraw consent to relinquishment
4. Bennett was incarcerated — outgoing prison mail takes minimum 48 hours to reach a local recipient, making withdrawal physically impossible
5. Agreement was NOT mentioned in or submitted with the relinquishment hearing paperwork
6. Relinquishment paperwork independently states relinquishment was in T.B.'s "best interest" — without disclosing the contact-based inducement

**Legal Significance:**
- RCW 26.33.160 requires consent to relinquishment be "voluntary"
- Consent procured by unenforceable promises with impossible withdrawal windows is not voluntary
- Demaray knew Bennett was incarcerated, knew the 48-hour window was impossible, knew the agreement was unenforceable
- This supports CR 60(b)(3) fraud upon the court and CR 60(b)(4) void judgment
- For § 1983: attorney acting in concert with Manning to deprive parental rights under color of legal process
