# Master Blueprint for Federal Civil Rights Complaint
**Case Name:** Zackariah Bennett v. Melvina Manning, et al.
**Jurisdiction:** U.S. District Court, Western District of Washington
**Primary Statutes:** 42 U.S.C. §§ 1983, 1985(3), 1986; 28 U.S.C. §§ 1367, 2201

---

## 1. THE FOUNDATIONAL FRAUD (2008)
**Factual Anchor:** The Dec 5, 2008 Findings of Fact (Case 08-3-03956-4SEA).
*   **The Contradiction:** Finding of "Willful Abandonment" (RCW 26.09.191) while simultaneously finding "Threat to Remove Child from State."
*   **The Fraud:** Attorney Nancy Gass presented these findings 9 days after Petitioner Manning swore Zack was calling "a couple times per week."
*   **Due Process Violation:** Default entered against an incarcerated person (in Colorado) despite a handwritten response (11/09/2008 letter) being entered into the record.

## 2. THE FRAUDULENT INDUCEMENT (2012-2013)
**Factual Anchor:** The 2012 Communication Agreement vs. The 2013 Snohomish Order (13-5-00050-3).
*   **The Bait:** Manning signed an agreement acknowledging Zack-TB contact was in the "Best Interests" of the child and promising 8 visits per year.
*   **The Switch:** Manning filed the Relinquishment but suppressed the Communication Agreement from the court record.
*   **The Conspiracy:** Used Snohomish County venue to bypass King County judges who had the 2008 file. The case was dismissed in 2019, meaning no final adoption ever legally replaced Zack's rights.

## 3. THE INTERCEPTION & ALIENATION (2013-2022)
**Factual Anchor:** "Read-to-Me-Daddy" tapes, Angel Tree gifts, and the 2017 concealment of brother A.B.
*   **The Tort:** Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress (IIED). Manning acknowledged receipt of Zack's prison outreach efforts but intentionally withheld them to manufacture a "No Contact" narrative for future court proceedings.
*   **Sibling Rights:** The active concealment of A.B.'s identity from TB constitutes a separate layer of emotional harm and interference.

## 4. THE SHIELDING FRAUD (2022-2026)
**Factual Anchor:** The 2022 DVPO and the 2026 Appellate Opinion.
*   **The Barrier:** Manning used a defective DVPO (based on 2008 lies) to trigger "Collateral Estoppel" against Zack's 2023 fraud lawsuit.
*   **The "Bargain":** Kelly’s admission ("Manning is holding up her end of the bargain") establishes the class-based or conspiratorial animus under § 1985(3).
*   **The Exhaustion:** The 2026 Opinion confirms that State Courts refuse to fund the record for indigent litigants, effectively granting immunity to those who commit fraud against the poor.
