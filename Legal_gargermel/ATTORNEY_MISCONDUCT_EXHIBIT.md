### II. EVIDENCE OF FRAUD IN THE INDUCEMENT (MARK M. DEMARAY)

**A. The "Impossible" Withdrawal Window (2013)**
Attorney Mark M. Demaray drafted the "Agreement and Order for Communication and Contact" (Case No. 13-5-00050-3).
- **The Provision:** Section 1.1 required Petitioner to notify both the Court and Manning within **48 hours** to withdraw consent to the relinquishment.
- **The Fraud:** Demaray knew Petitioner was **incarcerated** in a state facility where outgoing mail takes a minimum of 48 hours to reach a local recipient, making the right to withdraw legally and physically impossible to exercise.

**B. Unenforceability by Design**
The Agreement drafted by Demaray explicitly required a judge's signature to be enforceable (§1.1).
- **The Omission:** Demaray never obtained the judge's signature, and the agreement was not mentioned in or submitted with the formal relinquishment hearing paperwork.
- **The Inducement:** Petitioner relied on this "agreement" to sign the relinquishment, not knowing the attorney had left it unsigned and unenforceable.
