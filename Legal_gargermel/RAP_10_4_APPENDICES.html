Appendix A: Court of Appeals Opinion, No. 87007-6-I (Jan. 26, 2026)

Refer to the attached copy of the Unpublished Opinion in Case No. 87007-6-I.

The Court of Appeals affirmed the trial court's summary judgment. Critical findings for review include the court's assertion at pages 1 and 10 that Petitioner made "no contact" with the minor child between 2008 and 2019, and the court's refusal at page 4, footnote 1, to conduct a full de novo review due to the absence of transcripts—transcripts which the trial court refused to fund in violation of published precedent.

Appendix B: Order Denying Reconsideration (Feb. 18, 2026)

Refer to the attached Order from the Court of Appeals, Division One.

Petitioner moved for reconsideration identifying manifest errors of fact regarding admitted Exhibit A and manifest errors of law regarding the "indigency trap." The Court denied reconsideration without addressing these specific points.

Appendix H: Opinion, Bennett v. Manning, No. 84951-4-I (Apr. 17, 2023)

In this published decision, the Court of Appeals held that the trial court erred by failing to enter indigency findings under RAP 15.2(c) and by exceeding its authority in denying public funding. The trial court's failure to comply with the subsequent mandate created the "incomplete record" for which Petitioner was later penalized.

Appendix I: Exhibit A (Admitted 12/8/2025) Contact Summary

Exhibit A documents a continuous history of contact attempts and interactions from 2008 through 2019, including:

  • 2010–2011: Scheduled phone calls arranged by Manning.
  • 2011: Video visit at the Manning residence.
  • 2012: In-person visit and medical coordination.
  • 2012–2015: "Read-to-Me-Daddy" recordings and holiday gifts acknowledged by Manning.

These records were admitted into the appellate record but ignored in the final Opinion.
