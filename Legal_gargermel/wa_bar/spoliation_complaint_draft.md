# VERBATIM EVIDENCE OF SPOLIATION AND PROFESSIONAL MISCONDUCT
## Case Reference: Bennett v. Manning (King County #23-2-11352-7 / Appeal #87007-6)
## Target of Complaint: Nancy A. Gass (WSBA #49685)

### I. VERBATIM ADMISSION OF DESTRUCTION
On February 5, 2024, Nancy Gass admitted in writing that her client, Melvina Manning, failed to preserve original surveillance footage of the June 12, 2022 incident—the foundational event for the Domestic Violence Protection Order (DVPO) that Gass subsequently used to secure a dismissal of civil claims via *res judicata*.

**The Admission (Verbatim from Gass Email):**
> "We sent you the only video my client has. Her system deletes videos every 30 days. The only way she knew how to preserve the video was to record it on her cell phone."

### II. FACTUAL BASIS FOR CULPABILITY
1.  **Actual Knowledge:** Manning filmed the footage herself during an active legal proceeding she initiated. She was the petitioner; she had an absolute duty to preserve the primary evidence of her own claims.
2.  **Culpable Manipulation (The "Secondary Recording"):** Analysis of the produced video reveals it was recorded by a cellular phone pointed at a monitor on **07/09/2022 at 9:02 PM**—nearly a month after the incident. 
3.  **Selective Extraction:** Manning reviewed the original footage, selected a 93-second segment that served her narrative, recorded only that segment on her phone in low resolution (460x260), and then allowed the original high-resolution footage to be deleted.
4.  **Intentional Suppression of Context:** The original footage (as testified to by two eyewitnesses) contained evidence of Manning publicly slandering the Appellant and, crucially, **Manning instructing the Appellant to approach the porch.** By destroying the original and producing only a curated 93-second clip, Manning and Gass presented a "false light" narrative to multiple courts.

### III. VIOLATION OF RPC 3.3 (CANDOR TO THE TRIBUNAL)
Nancy Gass exacerbated this spoliation by:
*   **Failing to Disclose:** Producing the 93-second clip without initially disclosing it was a secondary recording of destroyed evidence.
*   **Misleading Arguments:** Using the resulting "harassment" finding from the DVPO court (which was deprived of the full video) as a hammer to bar all of Bennett's civil claims in Case #23-2-11352-7. 
*   **Actual Knowledge of Falsity:** Gass possessed the evidence refuting Manning's "total silence" narrative (the 2011 video visit and 2012 medical engagements) since February 2024, yet continued to rely on Manning's September 19, 2023 declaration asserting "Mr. Bennett never made any attempt to contact TB from 2008 until 2019."

### IV. LEGAL STANDARD (Washington Spoliation Doctrine)
Under Washington law, where evidence is unique (like surveillance footage of a specific date) and the spoliating party analyzed it before its destruction (as evidenced by the curated cell phone clip), the proper remedy is an adverse inference or default. By concealing this destruction and moving for summary judgment based on the "preclusive" effect of a proceeding tainted by that very destruction, Ms. Gass has violated her fundamental duty of candor and engaged in professional malpractice.
