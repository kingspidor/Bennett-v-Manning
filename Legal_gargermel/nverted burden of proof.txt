  the context matches your description of the court applying the CR 56 standard to the wrong party.

  Here are the specific instances where this violation is documented:


  1. Summary Judgment Inversion (CR 56 Violation)
   * Location: 87007-6-Motion-Reconsideration-20260207.pdf (Section IV.A: "BOTH COURTS INVERTED THE SUMMARY JUDGMENT
     STANDARD").
   * The Violation: You argue that the court failed to view the evidence in the light most favorable to the non-moving
     party (Appellant). Instead, the court accepted the Respondent’s "11-year silence" narrative as an undisputed fact,
     despite the presence of Exhibit A (the contact logs) which directly contradicted it.
   * Context: By resolving this factual dispute in favor of the party moving for dismissal (Manning), the court inverted
     the foundational requirement of CR 56, which mandates that any genuine issue of material fact must result in a
     denial of the motion.


  2. Failure of the Initial Burden of Production
   * Location: master_evidence_distill.md (Table 2, Row 8) and 87007-6-Brief-Reply-20250728.pdf (Section IX).
   * The Violation: You contend the Respondent failed to meet her initial burden to negate your claims. Under Washington
     law (Young v. Key Pharmaceuticals), the moving party must first demonstrate the absence of a genuine issue.
   * The Inversion: The court ignored that Manning’s motion was supported primarily by unsworn assertions of counsel and
     "extra-record" materials. By granting the motion anyway, the court improperly shifted the entire burden onto you to
     prove your case before the Respondent had legally earned the right to summary judgment.


  3. De Novo Review vs. Procedural Bar
   * Location: Manifest Errors - Sheet2.csv (Row 14).
   * The Violation: The Court of Appeals promised a de novo review (which requires looking at the record as if for the
     first time with no deference to the trial court).
   * The Inversion: Instead of performing a de novo review, the court applied a procedural bar (denying review due to
     the missing transcript). This effectively placed the burden on the indigent Appellant to provide a record that the
     court itself had blocked through the "Indigency Trap," thereby insulating the trial court’s errors from the
     required standard of review.


  4. Deemed Admissions under CR 8(d)
   * Location: wa_bar/spoliation_complaint_draft.md and master_evidence_distill.md (Table 2, Row 10).
   * The Violation: On November 24, 2025, the court ordered Manning to respond to your motion to supplement by December

   * The Inversion: Under CR 8(d), a failure to deny allegations in a required responsive pleading constitutes an
     admission. You argue the court inverted this rule by continuing to rule in Manning's favor, effectively granting
     her the benefit of her silence rather than treating your facts as admitted.


  Summary of the "Inverted" Context
