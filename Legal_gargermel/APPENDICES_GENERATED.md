### Appendix H: Opinion, Bennett v. Manning, No. 84951-4-I (April 17, 2023)
*Published Opinion (Div. One) — Judges Dwyer, Coburn, Andrus*

**Holding:**
- Reversed the trial court for failing to enter findings on indigency under RAP 15.2(c).
- Held that the final authority to deny public funding rests with the Supreme Court, not the trial court or commissioners.
- **Mandate:** Remanded for "proceedings consistent with this opinion." (The trial court never conducted the remanded hearing).

### Appendix I: Exhibit A (Evidence Admitted December 8, 2025)
*Documenting contact attempts and interactions 2008–2019*

**Key Evidence Disproving "11-Year Silence":**
1. **2008:** Sworn admission by Manning that Bennett called T.B. "couple times per week" (Declaration ¶4).
2. **2010–2011:** Facebook logs documenting "set times" for calls arranged by Manning.
3. **June 12, 2011:** Video of in-person visit at Manning home; 3-year-old T.B. identifies Bennett as "daddy."
4. **2012:** Documentation of medical engagement and multi-family visit.
5. **2012–2015:** Acknowledgments from Manning of "Read-to-Me-Daddy" recordings and "Angel Tree" gifts.
6. **2017:** Manning babysitting Bennett's younger son (A.B.) while concealing his identity from T.B.
